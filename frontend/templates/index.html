      How It Works

      1

      Upload

      Your video is sent in small chunks straight to disk, so even multi-gigabyte files never fill your browser's memory. Interrupted uploads resume automatically.

      2

      Inspect

      FFprobe reads the real contents of the file (not just its extension): resolution, frame rate, duration, and every audio track.

      3

      Convert

      FFmpeg resizes to 1080p while the audio stream is copied bit-for-bit whenever the container allows it. Hardware encoders are used when available.

      4

      Download

      Preview and download the real converted file. It is deleted automatically after the retention period, or immediately when you delete it.

      Resizing vs. AI enhancement

      Fast Upscaling uses a high-quality Lanczos resize. It makes the picture 1920×1080 and keeps edges clean, but it cannot recreate detail that was never recorded. AI Enhancement runs every frame through a neural network (Real-ESRGAN) that can sharpen edges, reduce compression blocks and add plausible detail. It needs a Vulkan-capable GPU, takes much longer, and results depend on the footage. It is entirely optional.

      About

      UpscaleHD is free, open and has no accounts, watermarks, subscriptions or payment walls. Free software is not unlimited hosting, though: the server operator sets the real limits on storage, bandwidth and processing speed. Currently: files up to 5 GB, converted files kept for 24 hours, and conversions processed in a queue. For the fastest results and no upload at all, run UpscaleHD on your own computer (see the README).
